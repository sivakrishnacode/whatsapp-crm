About Converse360

Built for businesses that talk to customers at scale

Converse360 is a customer communication platform that helps businesses manage conversations and customer interactions across multiple channels from one place. It brings together a shared team inbox, contact management, broadcast messaging, deal pipelines, workflow automations, and an AI assistant into a single dashboard.

Whether your team handles WhatsApp support, Instagram DMs, or web chat, Converse360 keeps every conversation in one organised view — so nothing falls through the cracks and customers always get a timely response.

Converse360 is operated by Conceps Media Works, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

Google Integrations

Connect Converse360 with Google services

Converse360 can integrate with Google services to help businesses manage communication and workflow automation. All Google integrations are entirely opt-in — no Google data is accessed unless a business explicitly connects their Google account from within the Converse360 settings.

📧
Gmail
Send email from your own Gmail address as part of a Converse360 workflow — order confirmations, appointment reminders, and follow-ups after a conversation. Converse360 only sends on your behalf; it does not read, search or store your mailbox.
📅
Google Calendar
Schedule appointments and check availability directly from a customer conversation. Create calendar events and send booking confirmations without leaving the inbox.
📋
Google Sheets
Log leads, orders and conversation outcomes to a spreadsheet you choose, look up a row to enrich a contact, or update rows as a deal progresses. Converse360 only touches the spreadsheet you paste the link to — it never lists or browses your Drive.
🎥
Google Meet
Create a Google Meet link for a customer call and send it straight into the conversation. Converse360 can only create new meeting spaces — it cannot see, join or record your existing meetings.
Data use commitment: Google user data accessed through these integrations is used solely to deliver the feature you have enabled. It is never used for advertising, never shared with third parties for their own purposes, and never used to train AI models. Converse360's use of Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect any Google integration at any time from Settings → Integrations in Converse360, or from your Google Account permissions page.
